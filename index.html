Finding sand in a new city shouldn't depend on knowing a local.

Today it does. Players ask around. Locals know two or three courts and keep the rest to themselves. Clubs list hours nowhere, cities bury them in PDFs, hotel courts never make it online at all. The courts already exist — they are just not written down anywhere.

SandSpot is writing them down. One registry, every court, checked by the people who play on them. What you searched above is the registry working today — small, real, and growing.

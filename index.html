The mission

Finding sand in a new city shouldn't depend on knowing a local.

Today it does. Players ask around. Locals know two or three courts and keep the rest to themselves. Clubs list hours nowhere, cities bury them in PDFs, hotel courts never make it online at all.

The courts already exist — they are just not written down anywhere.

SandSpot is writing them down: one registry, every court, checked by the people who play on them. 76 are on file today. The map you just moved is the registry, working.

Where this goes

  1. Today
    The registry. We track and verify courts; the record grows every week. Corrections welcome — the players know best.
  2. Next
    The map, everywhere. Full court pages: photos, hours, rules. A copy of the truth in every player's pocket.
  3. Then
    The game. Book court time, find players at your level, run tournaments. The playing layer comes after the map earns trust.
